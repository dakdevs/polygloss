# ADR-0030: Motion system

- **Status:** Accepted. Supersedes [ADR-0029](0029-product-motion.md): its two motions are re-gated here and kept (retuned, with exits); its "five to seven motions, two passed" limit, its "under Reduce Motion nothing moves" rule and its rejections of panel, section and tree motion are replaced.
- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Design:** [§11.16 Motion](../design.md#1116-motion-adr-0030), [§12.1 Budgets](../design.md#121-budgets-apple-silicon-release-build), [research: motion audit](../research/motion-audit.md)

## Context

After M6 the user asked for the app to feel high quality while it runs, naming how panels collapse, and for every animation skill to inform it. Six lenses were applied to an audit of about 45 state changes: find-animation-opportunities (the four-question gate), animate with animation-vocabulary, css-animations and motion-react (technique), emil-design-engineering with ui-skills (speed, one easing family, press feedback), animation-performance, and animation-accessibility. motion-brief shaped the per-surface table below (each row answers its questions); pick-ui-library kept the work on gpui-base and our own code, with no new dependency; ui-recording-timeline is how values are checked. The skills' "five to seven motions" cap is lifted at the user's request; the default for 100+/day and keyboard paths is still no.

M6 shipped two entrances (ADR-0029); everything else snaps, and inherited gpui-kit motion is uneven (dialogs 250 ms, toasts 400 ms, menus none, a Switch spring that overshoots).

GPUI 0.3.7 can fade (per primitive, through a wrapper's `Styled::opacity`; `Window::with_element_opacity` is crate-private and there is no group opacity), translate (a relative offset, or `with_element_offset` around a child's prepaint, which its hitboxes follow), clip (content masks) and transform SVG sprites (scale, rotate). It cannot scale a `div`, text or a subtree. Elements live in a per-frame arena that is cleared after each frame, so an element cannot be kept and painted again; an exit re-renders from data. A subtree cannot be made inert: a hitbox is `Normal`, `BlockMouse` or `BlockMouseExceptScroll`, so a copy is inert only if it registers no listeners, or sits under an occluding hitbox. Every animation frame redraws the window: taffy is cleared and every uncached view laid out again whatever animates, so a property costs only what it reflows downstream (the viewport's split/unified fit, wrap passes, `set_metrics`); a `.cached()` view is reused only while its bounds and content mask are unchanged. gpui's `with_animation`/`with_spring` read the wall clock; gpui-base's `transition`, `spring`, `Presence` and `animate_keyframes` read the executor clock, which tests step, but all settle on frame 1 whenever `App::reduce_motion()` is set. The viewport commits a collapse or a section change in O(log n) and rebuilds its hitboxes from each frame's display list, so it can animate rows by displacing paint over a committed model. Details: [research: motion audit](../research/motion-audit.md).

## Decision

### Rules

1. **Gate.** A motion passes only if its trigger is off the 100+/day and keyboard-flow paths, it has a purpose (spatial consistency, preventing a jarring change, state indication, feedback), it fits its duration token (a tens-per-day pointer path gets QUICK, MICRO or a distance-adaptive reveal capped at BASE), and it delays nothing. "It looks nicer" is not a purpose.
2. **Commit first.** The model, layout, focus, cursor, scroll anchor and hit-testing take their end state on the commit frame; motion only paints the way there. No input waits for or queues behind motion.
3. **Explicit initiator.** Every caller passes `Initiator::{Pointer, Keyboard, Programmatic}`; each motion lists the initiators that animate it (its trigger). Programmatic changes (restore, URL and MCP `focus`, follow-on-scroll, auto-collapse, jumps, refresh, repartition) always snap. `Window::last_input_was_keyboard` only classifies user input inside a handler.
4. **Interruptible.** A re-toggle (a mouse down on the running motion's own trigger, or its keyboard path where that animates) retargets from the sampled value; a reversal is shortened by the share already travelled (gpui-base's rule). These **settling inputs** end every other running motion on the frame they arrive, before they are handled: a key down (modifiers alone excluded) other than the motion's own shortcut, a mouse down outside the motion's trigger, a scroll wheel or trackpad scroll, a window resize or fullscreen change, a divider drag. Pointer movement, hover, mouse up and modifier changes never settle. One viewport reveal runs at a time.
5. **Executor clock, one sampler.** Polygloss motion runs on its own `Track` sampler over `background_executor().now()`; it follows the motion policy, not `App::reduce_motion()` directly, and copies gpui-base's retarget rule. gpui `with_animation`, `with_animations`, `with_spring` and `Animation::new` are banned in app and viewport code; gpui-base motion is used only for `MotionReveal` (Home rows). `tests/scripts/motion-tokens.test.ts` enforces both.
6. **Clock after commit.** A `Track` stamps its start on the commit frame and clamps its first step to one 60 Hz frame (`FIRST_STEP`, 16.667 ms), so a heavy commit (a layout switch, a section open) never eats the start of the motion.
7. **No animated size reflows content.** Only a panel frame's own width (its content laid out once, at the target width, and held) and `MotionReveal` on a small non-virtualized list animate a size. In-diff motion is painter displacement and clipping over the committed model; `MotionReveal` never runs on the viewport or a gpui-kit `Tree`.
8. **Opacity safety.** No group opacity: never fade a surface whose shadow or overlapping fills would show through, or a surface over content it does not cover opaquely; fade content inside an opaque container (a wrapper's `opacity`; in the viewport, each painted color's alpha), ramp a shadow by α³, or move instead. Never crossfade two copies: old content leaves on the commit frame. Scale only SVG icons.
9. **Exits.** What enters also exits, on the entrance's axis or as a fade in place, never toward an edge the entrance did not use, over the exit duration of its row below. An exiting surface is inert from the commit: its model state (focus, pane cycle, `j`/`k`, actions) is gone, and it is re-rendered each frame from a snapshot with no listeners, focus handles, tab stops or tooltips (see Exits below). Exits never play because a tab switch dropped the element from a frame.
10. **One entrance per container.** A container moves or its content moves, never both. No stagger.
11. **Pixels and frames.** Animated offsets are quantized to device pixels (`round(v · scale) / scale`). Frames are requested only while a motion is unsettled, none after it settles. No looping or ambient motion (busy spinners only, each with a text label).
12. **Perf is a gate.** Every animation frame of the full app meets the §12.1 motion budgets; a motion that misses ships as Snap in every policy (a fade costs the same frame as a slide in GPUI). M1 and M2, the motions the user named, are the exception: a miss goes to the user at the M7 gate with its profile, instead of shipping the width-snap fallback silently.

### Motion policy

| Policy      | When                                                        | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Full**    | macOS Reduce Motion off                                     | The tables below                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Reduced** | macOS Reduce Motion on                                      | Gentler, not zero: travel, displacement, rotation and scale are 0 on every frame; entrances and opened content fade in over QUICK; an exit that fades over an empty area fades out over MICRO; panel and disclosure closes snap (a fade would show what lies beneath through the closing content); transforms jump. Kit motion snaps |
| **Off**     | Screenshot, E2E and perf harnesses (`MotionPolicyOverride`) | Everything settles on its first frame, kit motion included                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

- A reduced variant's type (`Reduced::{Snap, Fade}`) has no geometry field, so a reduced motion that moves cannot be written.
- The policy is derived each frame, never stored: the harness override if set; else Reduced while `App::reduce_motion()`; else Full. In production only gpui-base's `apply_system_reduce_motion` writes that flag (through the app's `ReduceMotionSource`), so app motion and kit motion follow one reading; production never calls `cx.set_reduce_motion`, which would stop gpui-base following the system. Any override (Off, or a test's Full or Reduced) also sets the flag, so kit motion settles in every harness whatever app policy a test samples.
- Reduce Motion is followed live: `NSWorkspaceAccessibilityDisplayOptionsDidChangeNotification` (observed in `polygloss-platform`) re-runs `apply_system_reduce_motion` and redraws every main window; the activation re-read stays as a fallback. A switch to Reduced zeroes travel on that frame and lets opacity finish; Off settles everything; Full never restarts a settled motion.
- No setting: Reduce Motion in System Settings is the control.

### Panels (M1, M2)

- **Frame.** While a panel's track is unsettled, the page draws that panel outside gpui-kit's split: a plain row with a frame `p · W` wide (the panel's background and 1 pt divider drawn as the split draws them) beside the main column. W is the stored width, clamped as the split clamps it. The motion never writes `ResizableState`, so a drag-resized width survives; at settle the split returns (open) or the panel unmounts (closed), and the last motion frame matches the split's first frame.
- **Content.** Laid out at W, never at the frame's width; painted at `(p − 1) · W` (sidebar) or `(1 − p) · W` (threads panel) and clipped to the frame. A closing panel is inert from the commit: focus moves, the pane cycle skips it, and an occluding layer covers its frame.
- **Traffic lights.** Native and fixed. The sidebar's top row (its segmented control and toggle included) slides under them, as Finder's does.
- **Toolbar (M1).** With `f` the frame's live width and `I` = `TOOLBAR_INSET_HIDDEN` 88 (`CANVAS` 12 in fullscreen): the show-sidebar button sits at window x `I`, clipped at the main column's leading edge, so a closing sidebar uncovers it and an opening one covers it. Hit-testing follows the end state: during a close the button is live wherever it is painted (a click reopens from the sampled width); during an open it is inert. The toolbar's first item sits at window x `max(f + CANVAS, I + SM + CONTROLS)`: continuous, at rest exactly today's two layouts (`W + 12`; the button at 88 and the first item at 120), and never under the traffic lights (they end at 79).
- **Give-way.** For the whole motion the toolbar keeps the item set that fits the narrower of its start and target widths, the narrowest it gets during the motion; items only the wider end shows appear at settle, at once (give-way never animates). The threads panel sits under the toolbar and never changes it.
- **Viewport.** `hold_layout(target width)` from the commit frame until settle keeps split/unified, wrap, the columns and the measurement width of host blocks and the header-card prelude; card frames, right-aligned header controls, the sticky header and the scrollbar follow the live width; content wider than a card clips at its edge, and row tints run to the live card edge.

### Exits

An `Exit` keeps a snapshot (the data its surface renders, never an element) and its surface's inert render function, and re-renders the copy each frame at its sampled opacity until its track settles. Its hitbox, per surface:

| Exit                        | Hitbox                           | Why                                                                         |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| M1, M2 closing frame (Full) | Occluding layer over the frame   | A click in the shrinking frame does nothing; nothing usable lies beneath it |
| M9 closing body             | Occluding layer over its clip    | As above                                                                    |
| M12 notice                  | None                             | It fades only over an empty strip                                           |
| M14 Home row                | Occluding, over its closing slot | A click on the leaving row does nothing                                     |

### Feedback without motion (every policy)

- **Press:** every custom `div` control and every control the viewport paints (chevron, Viewed, ⋯, open in editor, gap links, band Show and Mark all viewed) shows the pressed ink on mouse-down and drops it on mouse-up or when the pointer leaves, with no transition. The viewport paints it as one quad in the hover pass from its existing `pressed` state. Kit buttons keep their own.
- **Hover** and the **focus ring** are instant. Selected, hover and pressed states change color only, never weight or padding (ADR-0031).

### Never animated

The line cursor and selection; `j`/`k`, `n`/`p`, `]`/`[`, `.`/`,`; every scroll-to-target jump, smooth scrolling and landing highlights; wheel and trackpad scrolling and sticky-header pinning; viewport rubber-banding (OQ-62); plain → highlighted token swaps and "Loading…" → rows; switching reviews (⌘0–⌘9, ⌃Tab, ⌘{ ⌘}); Find open, close and next; split ↔ unified content; accordion follow; counters, badges and `N/M`; toolbar give-way, on resize and at a panel motion's settle; panel drag-resize; hover, press and the focus ring; theme and appearance; live Refresh and iteration swaps; the header card's async load; the composer; Viewed toggles (`v`, the pill, the circle) and the auto-collapse with its jump; `z`, `e`, `E`; menus; ⌘K, ⌘P, `?`; looping or ambient motion (a pulsing Live dot); staggered lists; scaled `div` or text entrances.

### Verification

Each motion ships with sampled tests on the executor clock and a **stepping protocol**: the commit frame is t = 0; the test advances exactly `FIRST_STEP` and draws, then advances to each sampled t, so t is measured from the commit stamp and the first-step clamp never engages (its own test drives it). Full: the commit frame at full travel, a sample at half the duration strictly between and equal to the test's own evaluation of the curve, settled after the duration. `Reduced::Fade`: zero travel at every sample and opacity strictly between 0 and 1 at half the fade's duration. `Reduced::Snap`, Keyboard (unless listed), Programmatic and Off: settled on the commit frame, no frame requested after. Pointer movement mid-motion never settles it; each settling input does. Each motion also has a filmstrip baseline (Full and Reduced rows at t = 0, ¼, ½, ¾, 1, stepped the same way) and, on hot surfaces, an app-shell frame-time metric. Values are frozen only after a 60 fps recording is scrubbed with ui-recording-timeline; PANEL is calibrated against Finder's ⌃⌘S.

## Consequences

- Fifteen motions instead of two, all commit-first and interruptible; the threads panel's content entrance becomes M2 and the banner entrance M12 (160 → 150 ms, plus an exit).
- `motion::enter_from`, `ENTER_PANEL`, `ENTER_NOTICE`, `ease_out_quint` and `ease_out_cubic` are retired once their callers move; until then `enter_from` maps Reduced to Snap, as in M6.
- Harnesses switch from `cx.set_reduce_motion(true)` to the policy override; Reduce Motion now means fades, so a harness left on Reduced would capture mid-fade frames.
- The viewport gains a reveal mechanism (painter displacement), `hold_layout` and initiator-aware toggles; its existing methods keep their meaning (Programmatic, snap).
- While a panel moves, the page draws it outside gpui-kit's split; the split and its stored widths are untouched.
- ⌘K, ⌘P and `?` leave gpui-component's `Dialog` for an app-owned overlay.
- New perf metrics measure animation frames in the full app shell (the existing harness measures a bare viewport window).
- The PANEL value, keyboard panel toggles, toasts, the Snapshot label, rubber-banding and a pinned header's collapse stay open questions (OQ-58–OQ-63).
