# ADR-0030: Motion system

- **Status:** Accepted. Supersedes [ADR-0029](0029-product-motion.md): its two motions are re-gated here and kept (retuned, with exits); its "five to seven motions, two passed" limit, its "under Reduce Motion nothing moves" rule and its rejections of panel, section and tree motion are replaced.
- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Design:** [§11.16 Motion](../design.md#1116-motion-adr-0030), [§12.1 Budgets](../design.md#121-budgets-apple-silicon-release-build), [research: motion audit](../research/motion-audit.md)

## Context

After M6 the user asked for the app to feel high quality while it runs, naming how panels collapse, and for every animation skill to inform it. Six lenses were applied to an audit of about 45 state changes: find-animation-opportunities (the four-question gate), animate with animation-vocabulary, css-animations and motion-react (technique), emil-design-engineering with ui-skills (speed, one easing family, press feedback), animation-performance, and animation-accessibility. motion-brief shaped the per-surface table below (each row answers its questions); pick-ui-library kept the work on gpui-base and our own code, with no new dependency; ui-recording-timeline is how values are checked. The skills' "five to seven motions" cap is lifted at the user's request; the default for 100+/day and keyboard paths is still no.

M6 shipped two entrances (ADR-0029); everything else snaps, and inherited gpui-kit motion is uneven (dialogs 250 ms, toasts 400 ms, menus none, a Switch spring that overshoots).

GPUI 0.3.7 can fade (per primitive, through a wrapper's `Styled::opacity`; `Window::with_element_opacity` is crate-private and there is no group opacity), translate (a relative offset, or `with_element_offset` around a child's prepaint, which its hitboxes follow), clip (content masks) and transform SVG sprites (scale, rotate). It cannot scale a `div`, text or a subtree. Elements live in a per-frame arena cleared after each frame, so an element cannot be kept and painted again; an exit re-renders from data. Element state (a div's scroll offset and pending click, `use_keyed_state`, a kit Button's focus handle) is keyed by the element's whole id path, and a view adds its entity id to the path without resetting it, so a subtree whose parents change loses its state. A subtree cannot be made inert: a hitbox is `Normal`, `BlockMouse` or `BlockMouseExceptScroll`, so a copy is inert only if it registers no listeners, or sits under an occluding hitbox. Key-down listeners, capture phase included, run only after action bindings; only keystroke interceptors (`App::intercept_keystrokes`) run before them, and they also see a lone modifier's release as a keystroke named `shift`, `control`, `alt`, `platform` or `function`. Mouse listeners are registered during paint and last one frame. Every animation frame redraws the window: taffy is cleared and every uncached view laid out again whatever animates, so a property costs only what it reflows downstream (the viewport's split/unified fit, wrap passes, `set_metrics`). A `.cached()` view is reused while its bounds, content mask and text style are unchanged, and reuse replays its recorded scene at the opacity it was painted with. gpui's `with_animation`/`with_spring` read the wall clock; gpui-base's `transition`, `spring`, `Presence` and `animate_keyframes` read the executor clock, which tests step, but all settle on frame 1 whenever `App::reduce_motion()` is set. The viewport commits a collapse or a section change in O(log n) and rebuilds its hitboxes from each frame's display list, so it can animate rows by displacing paint over a committed model. A strong ease-out front-loads travel: `cubic_bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` covers 37% of a 240 ms motion in its first 60 Hz frame and 97% by its midpoint. Details: [research: motion audit](../research/motion-audit.md).

## Decision

### Rules

1. **Gate.** A motion passes only if its trigger is off the 100+/day and keyboard-flow paths, it has a purpose (spatial consistency, preventing a jarring change, state indication, feedback), it fits its duration token (a tens-per-day pointer path gets QUICK, MICRO or a distance-adaptive reveal capped at BASE), and it delays nothing. "It looks nicer" is not a purpose.
2. **Commit first.** The model, layout, focus, cursor, scroll anchor and hit-testing take their end state on the commit frame; motion only paints the way there. No input waits for or queues behind motion. The one deferral is a Reduced close (Motion policy): its frame or slot keeps its size, inert, for MICRO while its content fades.
3. **Explicit initiator.** Every caller passes `Initiator::{Pointer, Keyboard, Programmatic}`; each motion lists the initiators that animate it (its trigger). Programmatic changes (restore, URL and MCP `focus`, follow-on-scroll, auto-collapse, jumps, refresh, repartition) always snap. `Window::last_input_was_keyboard` only classifies user input inside a handler.
4. **Interruptible.** A re-toggle (a click on the running motion's own trigger, or its keyboard path where that animates) retargets from the sampled value. A **key down** (a lone modifier excluded) other than the motion's own shortcut, a **scroll**, a **window resize** or a **fullscreen change** settles every running motion before it is handled. A **mouse down** outside the motion's trigger **freezes** every running motion at its sampled value (paint and hit-testing hold still) and settles it once the matching mouse up has been dispatched, so a click mid-motion hits what was painted where it was pressed. Pointer movement, hover and modifier changes never settle. A divider takes no drag while its panel moves. One viewport reveal runs at a time.
5. **Executor clock, one sampler.** Polygloss motion runs on its own `Track` sampler over `background_executor().now()`; it follows the motion policy, not `App::reduce_motion()` directly, and copies gpui-base's retarget rule. gpui `with_animation`, `with_animations`, `with_spring` and `Animation::new` are banned in app and viewport code; gpui-base motion is used only for `MotionReveal` (Home rows, driven by our track). `tests/scripts/motion-tokens.test.ts` enforces both.
6. **Clock after commit.** A `Track` stamps its start on the commit frame and clamps its first step to one 60 Hz frame (`FIRST_STEP`, 16.667 ms), so a heavy commit (a layout switch, a section open) never eats the start of the motion.
7. **No animated size reflows content.** Only a panel frame's own width (its content laid out once, at the panel's width, and held) and `MotionReveal` on a small non-virtualized list animate a size. In-diff motion is painter displacement and clipping over the committed model; `MotionReveal` never runs on the viewport or a gpui-kit `Tree`.
8. **Opacity safety.** No group opacity: never fade a surface whose shadow or overlapping fills would show through, or a surface over content it does not cover opaquely; fade content inside an opaque container (a wrapper's `opacity`; in the viewport, each painted color's alpha), ramp a shadow by α³, or move instead. Never crossfade two copies. A `.cached()` view renders uncached while an opacity or offset over it is unsettled (a replayed scene keeps the opacity it was recorded at). Scale only SVG icons.
9. **Exits.** What leaves its place exits toward the edge it entered from, or fades in place, over the exit duration of its row below. Content swapped inside a frame that stays (a step's content, a replaced notice, the chip that replaces a card, the camera icon's return) leaves on the commit frame (rule 8: no crossfade). An exiting surface is inert from the commit: its model state (focus, pane cycle, `j`/`k`, actions) is gone. A copy that outlives its model is re-rendered each frame from a snapshot with no listeners, focus handles, tab stops or tooltips (Exits, below); live content that leaves (a closing panel, the accordion's closing body) stays mounted under an occluding layer instead. Exits never play because a tab switch dropped the element from a frame.
10. **One entrance per container.** A container moves or its content moves, never both. No stagger.
11. **Pixels and frames.** Animated geometry is quantized once per frame to device pixels (`round(v · scale) / scale`), and every edge derived from it is computed from that value (a panel's frame width, its content's offset, its divider and the main column's edge share one `f`). Frames are requested only while a motion is unsettled, none after it settles. No looping or ambient motion (busy spinners only, each with a text label).
12. **Perf is a gate.** Every animation frame of the full app meets the §12.1 motion budgets; a motion that misses ships as Snap in every policy (a fade costs the same frame as a slide in GPUI). M1 and M2, the motions the user named, are the exception: a miss goes to the user at the M7 gate with its profile, instead of shipping the width-snap fallback silently.
13. **Stable identity.** No motion changes an element's parents: a surface keeps one element tree, and one id path, on the frames before, during and after its motion, so element state survives every frame. Motion wrappers carry no element id. Motion state lives in the entity that owns the surface (a `Track` field), never in keyed element state.

### Motion policy

| Policy      | When                                                        | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Full**    | macOS Reduce Motion off                                     | The tables below                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Reduced** | macOS Reduce Motion on                                      | Gentler, not zero: travel, displacement, rotation and scale are 0 on every frame. Entrances and opened content fade in over QUICK inside their final frame; exits, closes and collapses fade out over MICRO inside their unchanged frame or slot, which then snaps (nothing beneath shows through). Kit motion snaps |
| **Off**     | Screenshot, E2E and perf harnesses (`MotionPolicyOverride`) | Everything settles on its first frame, kit motion included                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

- A reduced variant's type (`Reduced { enter, exit }`, each `Snap` or `Fade`) has no geometry field, so a reduced motion that moves cannot be written.
- The policy is derived each frame, never stored: the harness override if set; else Reduced while `App::reduce_motion()`; else Full. In production only gpui-base's `apply_system_reduce_motion` writes that flag (through the app's `ReduceMotionSource`), so app motion and kit motion follow one reading; production never calls `cx.set_reduce_motion`, which would stop gpui-base following the system. Any override (Off, or a test's Full or Reduced) also sets the flag, so kit motion settles in every harness whatever app policy a test samples; clearing the override restores the flag it found.
- Reduce Motion is followed live: `NSWorkspaceAccessibilityDisplayOptionsDidChangeNotification` (observed in `polygloss-platform`) re-runs `apply_system_reduce_motion` and redraws every main window; the activation re-read stays as a fallback. A switch to Reduced zeroes travel on that frame and lets opacity finish; Off settles everything; Full never restarts a settled motion.
- No setting: Reduce Motion in System Settings is the control.

### Viewport reveals (M3–M8)

One formula drives the curtain, the card's frame and everything below it. A body (a file's, a section's, a gap's inserted rows, a block, the prelude) changes from h₀ to h₁ on the commit frame; Δ = h₁ − h₀, and the travel is clamped, D = min(abs(Δ), viewport height). With `s = SLIDE(t / duration)`, the body's visible height is `h(t) = h₀ + D · s` while expanding and `h(t) = h₁ + D · (1 − s)` while collapsing; at settle it is h₁. The curtain sits at the body's top + h(t); the card's frame (bottom padding, border, corners, shadow) closes at the curtain; every later slot is painted at its committed y + (h(t) − h₁), so the next card's top is always the frame's bottom + `CARDS`. Whatever the clamp skips lies beyond the viewport's bottom on both ends. The body's rows keep the screen y they had on the frame before the commit: for a pinned header the commit moves the anchor to the header, and the rows that were on screen stay where they were under the curtain while the next card rises from the viewport's bottom (OQ-63). Hit-testing follows the painted frame; the model, scrollbar and cursor are final from the commit.

### Panels (M1, M2)

- **Split.** Both panels are app-owned splits, one element tree in every state: a frame `f` wide holding the panel's content, a 1 pt divider on the frame's inner edge, and the main column. Nothing under the panel or the main column changes parents when the panel opens, moves or closes (rule 13). W is the stored width (per window for the sidebar, per tab for the threads panel; the ranges and clamps of today's gpui-kit splits), dragged on the divider's 9 pt band at rest. gpui-kit's `h_resizable` leaves the shell and the review body.
- **Frame.** W̄ = W + `BORDER` (the panel and its divider). Each frame, `f = quantize(p · W̄)` once; the main column starts at `f` (sidebar) or ends `f` from the window's right edge (threads panel); the divider is the frame's last (sidebar) or first (threads panel) point. At rest `f` is W̄ (open) or 0 (closed: the content unmounts).
- **Content.** Laid out at W, never at the frame's width; offset by `f − W̄` toward the panel's edge and clipped to the frame, so it rides the divider. A closing panel is inert from the commit (focus moves, the pane cycle skips it, an occluding layer covers its frame) and stays live under that layer: panels are rule 9's live-content case, not a snapshot. A Reduced fade of a panel's content, inside its opaque frame, lets the panel's own overlapping fills (the selected segment over its track, a row highlight under its text) show through each other for its 150 or 100 ms; that is accepted, and the segmented fill's shadow ramps by α³ (rule 8).
- **Traffic lights.** Native and fixed. The sidebar's top row (its segmented control and toggle included) slides under them, as Finder's does.
- **Toolbar (M1).** With `I` = `TOOLBAR_INSET_HIDDEN` 88 (`CANVAS` 12 in fullscreen): the show-sidebar button sits at window x `I`, clipped at the main column's leading edge, so a closing sidebar uncovers it and an opening one covers it. Hit-testing follows the end state: during a close the button is live wherever it is painted (a click reopens from the sampled width); during an open it is inert. The toolbar's first item sits at window x `max(f + CANVAS, I + SM + CONTROLS)`: continuous, at rest exactly today's two layouts (W̄ + 12 = 293 for W 280; the button at 88 and the first item at 120), and never under the traffic lights (they end at 79).
- **Give-way.** For the whole motion the toolbar keeps the item set that fits the narrower of its start and target widths, the narrowest it gets during the motion; items only the wider end shows appear at settle, at once (give-way never animates). The threads panel sits under the toolbar and never changes it.
- **Viewport.** `hold_layout(target width)` from the commit frame until settle holds only what reshapes: the split/unified choice, the wrap width, gutter digits and the measurement widths of host blocks and the header-card prelude. Everything else follows the live width without reshaping: card frames, the split halves (the right half starts at the live inner x plus half the live inner width; each half's content clips at its own edge), right-aligned header controls, the sticky header, the scrollbar and row tints.

### Exits

An `Exit` keeps a snapshot (the data its surface renders, never an element), its surface's inert render function, a placement and one track per channel, and re-renders the copy each frame until its longest channel settles; only then does it return `None`.

| Exit          | Placement                                                     | Channels                                                   | Hitbox                   | Why                                                              |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| M12 notice    | Overlay, absolutely placed at the strip's bounds              | Opacity and y on one track, MICRO (Reduced: opacity only)  | None                     | It leaves only over an empty strip                               |
| M14 Home row  | In flow, in its slot, wrapped in `MotionReveal(height share)` | Height `exit(BASE)` 150 and opacity MICRO; it lives 150 ms | Occluding, over its slot | The rows below follow the slot; a click on the copy does nothing |

A closing panel and the accordion's closing body are live content under an occluding layer (rule 9), not `Exit`s.

### Feedback without motion (every policy)

- **Press:** every custom `div` control and every control the viewport paints (chevron, Viewed, ⋯, open in editor, gap links, band Show and Mark all viewed) shows the pressed ink on mouse-down and drops it on mouse-up or when the pointer leaves, with no transition. The viewport paints it as one quad in the hover pass from its existing `pressed` state. Kit buttons keep their own.
- **Hover** and the **focus ring** are instant. Selected, hover, focused and pressed states change color only, never weight, width or padding (ADR-0031).

### Verification

Each motion ships with sampled tests on the executor clock and a **stepping protocol**: the commit frame is t = 0; the test advances exactly `FIRST_STEP` and draws, then advances to each sampled t, so t is measured from the commit stamp and the first-step clamp never engages (its own test drives it). Full: the commit frame at full travel; the first frame after it at the curve's value for 16.667 ms; a sample at half the duration strictly between; settled after the duration. Expected values are the test's own evaluation of the curve, quantized as rule 11 quantizes (`round(v · scale) / scale`, written in the test). Reduced fades: zero travel at every sample and opacity strictly between 0 and 1 at half the fade's duration. Snaps, Keyboard (unless listed), Programmatic and Off: settled on the commit frame, no frame requested after. Pointer movement mid-motion never settles it; each settling input does, and a mouse down freezes it until the mouse up. Each motion also has a **filmstrip** baseline (Full and Reduced rows: the commit frame, the next three 60 Hz frames, ½ and 1 of the duration, so the frames where an ease-out moves most are always seen) and, on hot surfaces, an app-shell frame-time metric. Values are frozen only after a 60 fps recording is scrubbed with ui-recording-timeline; PANEL and SLIDE are calibrated together against Finder's ⌃⌘S (the share of the distance covered at each of the first four frames and at the midpoint, read from both recordings).

## Consequences

- Fifteen motions instead of two, all commit-first and interruptible; the threads panel's content entrance becomes M2 and the banner entrance M12 (160 → 150 ms, plus an exit).
- `motion::enter_from`, `ENTER_PANEL`, `ENTER_NOTICE`, `ease_out_quint` and `ease_out_cubic` are retired once their callers move; until then `enter_from` maps Reduced to Snap, as in M6.
- Harnesses switch from `cx.set_reduce_motion(true)` to the policy override; Reduce Motion now means fades, so a harness left on Reduced would capture mid-fade frames.
- The viewport gains a reveal mechanism (painter displacement), `hold_layout` and initiator-aware toggles; its existing methods keep their meaning (Programmatic, snap).
- The sidebar and the threads panel become app-owned splits; gpui-kit's `h_resizable` and its ratio-rescaling workarounds leave the shell and the review body.
- Motion settling is app-wide: a keystroke interceptor, a root element painted first in every main window (capture-phase mouse and scroll listeners) and a window-bounds observer.
- ⌘K, ⌘P and `?` leave gpui-component's `Dialog` for an app-owned overlay.
- New perf metrics measure animation frames in the full app shell (the existing harness measures a bare viewport window).
- The PANEL value and the SLIDE curve, keyboard panel toggles, toasts, the Snapshot label, rubber-banding and the pinned-header collapse stay open questions (OQ-58–OQ-63).
